# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions, and chat — is expected to be respectful, patient, and constructive. We welcome contributors of every background and skill level.

Examples of behavior that creates a welcoming environment:

- Using inclusive and friendly language.
- Gracefully accepting and giving constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing other people's private information without consent, and any sexualized language or imagery.

## Scope

This standard applies within project spaces (repository, issue tracker, PRs) and whenever an individual represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers through GitHub's private contact for this repository, or by opening a private security-style report if public disclosure would cause harm. All reports are reviewed and handled with discretion.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for serious or repeated violations.
